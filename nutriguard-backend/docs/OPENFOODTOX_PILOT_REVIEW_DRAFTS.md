# OpenFoodTox pilot -- EN/BG review drafts (concise samples, for Codex review)

Generated by `python3 -m scripts.openfoodtox.pilot` against `staging/v4`
(see `docs/OPENFOODTOX_PILOT_REPORT.md` §4-§6 for the full account). Committed
here, in full, for all four pilot identities so Codex can review through Git
without needing the VM's bulk `pilot/v2/` output directory (per
`docs/OPENFOODTOX_PILOT_REVIEW_TASK.md` item 5).

**Every draft below is DRAFT -- not scientifically reviewed, not
translation-reviewed, not publication-ready.** The EN and BG text are
independent, readable paraphrases built only from structured extracted
fields (value type, magnitude, unit, population, chemical basis, dates,
feed-context flag) -- never an algorithmic rewrite of the source's own
free-text justification. The source's own verbatim text is quoted separately
under "Internal evidence" for each identity, so a reviewer can check the
paraphrase against it; the two are never conflated, and the English quote is
never itself presented as a completed BG description. Chemical/unit/
population terms are deliberately left untranslated in the BG paraphrase
rather than guessed at (see the report's §6/§8).

"Intake guidance" and "What it is"/"Purpose in food" sections are hidden from
every draft below (no sourced content for the first two; gated on
scientific review, which stays `not_reviewed`, for the third) -- this is
recorded internally in each profile's `omitted_sections_internal_note`, not
silently dropped.

## E250 -- Sodium Nitrite

**Outcome: `ready_for_human_review`. The one reference value below is
`consumer_guidance_eligible` (see the report §3c/§5) -- the only one of all
four pilot substances that is.**

### EN draft

> EFSA set Acceptable Daily Intake (ADI) of 0.1 mg/kg bw/day for the general
> consumers population, with a chemical basis of sodium nitrite. (Source:
> OPENFOODTOX_Re-evaluation of potassium nitrite (E 249) and sodium nitrite
> (E 250) as food additives, 2017-04-05.)

### BG draft

> ЕФСА определя допустима дневна доза (ADI) от 0.1 mg/kg bw/day за общата
> популация от потребители, с химична основа sodium nitrite. (Източник:
> OPENFOODTOX_Re-evaluation of potassium nitrite (E 249) and sodium nitrite
> (E 250) as food additives, 2017-04-05.)

### Sources

- OPENFOODTOX_Re-evaluation of potassium nitrite (E 249) and sodium nitrite
  (E 250) as food additives (2017-04-05) -- doi:10.2903/j.efsa.2017.4786

### Internal evidence (verbatim, not for publication)

> "Remarks: The Panel concluded that an increased methaemoglobin level,
> observed in human and animals, was a relevant effect for the derivation of
> the ADI. Using the lowest BMDL of 9.63 mg/kg bw per day, and applying the
> default UF of 100, the Panel derived an ADI of 0.1 mg sodium nitrite/kg bw
> per day, corresponding to 0.07 mg nitrite ion/kg bw per day. The exposure
> to nitrite resulting from its use as food additive did not exceed this ADI
> for the general population, except for a slight exceedance in children at
> the highest percentile."

## E951 -- Aspartame

**Outcome: `ready_for_human_review`, but zero of five reference values are
`consumer_guidance_eligible`** -- every assessment states the ADI as, e.g.,
"40 mg/kg bw/day" without repeating the substance name immediately after
"mg" (the specific textual shape `chemical_basis.py` requires), so the basis
is honestly `unresolved_no_mention` in all five -- a dataset/text-shape
limitation, not a code defect (see the report §3c). The ADI itself (40
mg/kg bw/day) is reaffirmed identically across all five assessments
(2006-2013); a sixth dossier (2011-02-03 statement) carried no reference
value at all.

### EN draft (one representative paragraph; all five are structurally identical except for date/source -- full text in the profile file)

> EFSA set Acceptable Daily Intake (ADI) of 40 mg/kg bw/day for the general
> consumers population (the figure's exact chemical basis was not resolved
> from the source text). (Source: OPENFOODTOX_Scientific Opinion on the
> re-evaluation of aspartame (E 951) as a food additive, 2013-11-28.)

### BG draft

> ЕФСА определя допустима дневна доза (ADI) от 40 mg/kg bw/day за общата
> популация от потребители (точната химична основа на стойността не е
> установена от текста на източника). (Източник: OPENFOODTOX_Scientific
> Opinion on the re-evaluation of aspartame (E 951) as a food additive,
> 2013-11-28.)

### Sources (all five assessments)

- 2006-05-03 -- doi:10.2903/j.efsa.2006.356
- 2009-01-29 -- doi:10.2903/j.efsa.2009.945
- 2009-03-19 -- doi:10.2903/j.efsa.2009.1015
- 2011-02-25 -- doi:10.2903/j.efsa.2011.2089
- 2013-11-28 -- doi:10.2903/j.efsa.2013.3496 (the dataset's most recent;
  **a newer, 2026-09-10 opinion exists and is not in this dataset -- see the
  report §9**)

### Internal evidence (verbatim, 2013-11-28 opinion -- not for publication)

> "Remarks: The Panel concluded that aspartame was not of safety concern at
> the current aspartame exposure estimates or at the ADI of 40 mg/kg bw/day.
> Therefore, there was no reason to revise the ADI of aspartame. Current
> exposures to aspartame - and its degradation product
> 5-benzyl-3,6-dioxo-2-piperazine acetic acid (DKP) - were below their
> respective ADIs. The ADI is not applicable to Phenylketonuria (PKU)
> patients. These individuals require total control of dietary phenylalanine
> intake to manage the risk from elevated phenylalanine plasma levels."
